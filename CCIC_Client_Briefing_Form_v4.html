Industry Partnership, Collaboration and Training

Central Coast Industry Connect (CCIC) is the region's peak manufacturing network. As a CCIC Industry Partner, your business gains access to networking events, advisory support, and supplier connections — with SME members receiving a 10% discount on Food Manufacturing Innovation Hub facility use and training programs.

Program Questionnaire
Connecting students with real manufacturing environments to build the region's talent pipeline.
Fast-track induction for casual and temporary staff covering safety, hygiene, and core operating procedures.
Structured on-floor familiarisation for new operators across core production tasks and equipment.
Practical training for machine operators in setup, operation, minor maintenance, and troubleshooting.
Helps businesses identify growth opportunities, test ideas, and build innovation capacity across product and process.
Core leadership skills for team leaders and supervisors — communication, people development, and leading on the floor.
A development pathway for emerging leaders preparing the next generation of manufacturing managers.

Specialist Training Programs
